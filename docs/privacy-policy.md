## The Short Version

Hatch does not collect, transmit, or sell any personal data. Your tasks live on your device. There are no servers you are required to use and no way for us to see your data.

---

## What We Store and Where

Hatch saves two plain text files on your device:

- **tasks.json** — your tasks and custom lists
- **settings.json** — your theme preference, mascot position, and app preferences

Both files are stored in `%LocalAppData%\Hatch\` on your Windows device. They never leave your device unless you explicitly enable optional sync in Settings.

---

## Optional Sync

If you choose to enable sync (Settings → Sync), your task and list data is sent to a backend server using credentials you provide. This feature:

- Is **off by default** and requires explicit sign-in to activate
- Only sends your tasks and lists — never your device information or usage data
- Can be disabled at any time from Settings
- Allows you to delete your remote data at any time from Settings

We do not share your sync data with any third party.

---

## Analytics and Telemetry

**None.** Hatch contains no analytics SDK, no crash reporter, no feature-flag system, and no usage pings of any kind. We have no way to know how you use the app.

---

## Internet Access

Hatch requests internet access solely to support the optional sync feature described above. The app is fully functional with no internet connection at all times. No outbound network calls are made without your explicit action.

---

## Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your data with any third party, advertiser, or analytics provider.

---

## Data Deletion

- **Settings → Delete all my data** — removes all locally stored files immediately. No account or support request needed.
- **Uninstalling the app** — removes all app data via standard Windows MSIX cleanup.
- **Sync data** — can be deleted from Settings → Sync at any time.

---

## Children's Privacy

Hatch does not knowingly collect data from children. The app contains no social features, no user-generated public content, and no communication features.
